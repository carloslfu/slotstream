Use the already proposed engineering acceptance limits for the final comparison of the original affine four-bit checkpoint and the preselected minmax affine three-bit expert artifact: an upper bound on loss strictly below two percentage points overall and five in every family. Each of instruction following, restricted coding repair, factual choice, multilingual arithmetic and complete offline tool conversations carries one fifth of the overall weight. These limits are an implementation choice for this fixed comparison, not a universal definition of equal model quality.

Use the preselected fixed-weight MOVER combination of paired Tango stratum bounds. Allocate a one-sided alpha of 0.05 across the five family assertions and the overall assertion. This is an asymptotic paired method, not an exact finite-sample coverage certificate; the instrument checks and numerical diagnostics do not remove that limitation. All family gates must pass. Structural safety and execution integrity remain independent hard gates.

Freeze exact sources, independent task groups, prompts, graders, sample order, settings and cost before generating answers, as preserved in [[sources/runs/2026/10/2026-10-04-prospective-heldout-outcome-protocol]]. Exclude the entire group of any pilot case; treat translations of one arithmetic problem as one unit. Use all available eligible independent post-pilot tasks in four families and one thousand fixed hash-selected factual tasks. The small pilot does not establish sufficient power. An inconclusive result means not qualified, with no automatic sample extension, alternative-method selection or margin relaxation.

Both arms receive the same fourteen-GB target, 32K context, full 4,096-token reply reservation and two streamed drafts. Tool limits are twelve model steps per user turn, eight calls per step and ninety-six per complete case. Independent cases reset retained state; the fixed session cutoff permits reload only between completed cases. Preserve every task failure and complete executed history. A native, grader, identity, process-memory, pressure, wall or disk failure leaves the job incomplete and blocks analysis; never recode it as a model failure or replay a launched job.

The experiment has a seventy-two-hour cumulative active-job limit and six GB of new receipts within the existing total staging limit, with one model at a time and no new weights, raw logits, paid services or external tool actions. It does not qualify images, long context, ordinary app workflows, memory, speed or distribution. Those gates remain required under [[records/plan/same-model-quantization-and-automatic-memory-2026-10-02]].
