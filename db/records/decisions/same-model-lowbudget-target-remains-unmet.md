This is a conclusion about tested practical options, not all future methods. Never populate that set merely to claim completion. Do not weaken installation authentication, activation/rollback, saved ceilings or request-boundary rules to compensate for a performance miss.

- Keep the same checkpoint and publish measured speed tiers, with the twenty-token claim limited to its tested configurations and lower budgets honestly slower.

Require a cheap positive screen and a specific plausible contribution to the remaining gap before implementation or another large export. This is further research with an uncertain outcome, not a delivery promise.
