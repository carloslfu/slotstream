# Release calibration evidence

These are preserved observations and prospective protocols, not a new qualified performance baseline. See the canonical measurement record for exclusions and remaining work.

- `decode22/`: the incomplete original 22 GB experiment, including exact requests, wire responses, metrics, resource samples, plans and cleanup receipts.
- `smoke/`: the separate original functional pilot, excluded from timing calibration.
- `idle-smoke-v2/` and `idle-smoke-v2-attempt2/`: host-load readiness refusals. Neither started a model.
- `protocol.json`: original prospective protocol. `idle-protocol-v2.json`: stricter prospective host-load and live-plan protocol. The original verdicts are not rewritten.
- `population-policies.json`: keeps the incomplete active-Mac population out of idle calibration.
- `harness/`, `harness-idle-v2/`, `probe.swift`, `fixtures/`: exact source and public prompt fixtures. Original absolute runtime coordinates and hashes remain in the protocols.
- `historical-0.2.19-protocol.json`: evidence that the old 15.86 tok/s reference used a different workspace and prefix-cache configuration.
- `analysis.json`, `requests.csv`, `descriptive-summary.json`, `calibration-analysis.json`, `capture-check.json`: regenerated analysis. Descriptive v1 rates are not idle calibration.
- `manifest.json`: per-file byte hashes, excluding this manifest itself.
