**P1 capture qualification (C01, C02, C03).** Six validation requests, one per family kind, were generated twice in fresh processes, collector off and collector on: 6 requests, 1,218 outputs, identical output IDs, router digests and finish reasons on every request. **Training.** 12,540 train and 3,075 validation positions. Recipe as frozen: balanced multilabel BCE, AdamW 3e-4, weight decay 1e-4, gradient clip 1.0, batch 512, at most 20 epochs, patience 3 on validation loss, seed 17, 8 GB physical-footprint ceiling (peak 5.01 GB). G64 (2,416,896 parameters) stopped at epoch 4 with recall@16 0.082; G128 (4,809,216) stopped at epoch 10 with recall@16 0.109; both below the per-layer training-frequency prior (0.121). At the plan's nonconvergence rate 1e-3, G64, G128 and G256 all converged to the same trajectory (validation loss 0.6325, recall@16 0.121), which is exactly the frequency prior: the start-feature path contributes nothing a width change can fix. L128 (398,784 parameters, completed-layer `x2` plus layer and horizon embeddings) reached recall@16 0.193 at horizon 1 and 0.194 at horizon 4 after 20 epochs, still improving; at 1e-3 it collapsed to 0.078. A nested 28-request prefix gave 0.162 at horizon 1, so doubling the data added 3.1 points of recall.

**Offline evaluation (calibrated replay, 1,025 validation passes).** Bounded scheduler with the native settings (32-record cap, 8 lanes, 4-layer window), demand service time calibrated from the recorded events at 1025 passes; baseline demand I/O 37.0% of decode. The perfect-future oracle under the same bounds covers 100.0% of miss bytes in time and projects 1.59x (99.7% coverage even at twice the service time). Under the 1.20x traffic bound the best admission settings found on validation were: frequency prior None; recent routes 2 at 0.0% coverage; G64 top2-q0.95 at 0.5% coverage, precision 2.3%; G128 top2-q0.99 at 0.5% coverage, precision 2.8%; L128 top4-q0.99 at 1.4% coverage, precision 7.8%, projected 1.0052x; G128 with L128 refinement top4-q0.99 at 1.5% coverage, projected 1.0055x. Without the traffic bound the best any policy reached was 16.2% coverage at 4.89x file traffic, projecting 1.064x. The frozen offline continuation rule therefore reads: stop: no policy projects at least 1.05x under the traffic bound.

**Native exactness of the prefetch path (C06).** The six correctness requests were regenerated with prefetch on (G128 pack, top 32 per layer, 32-record cap, 128 MiB reserve charged by the planner, 4206 slots) and compared with the collector-off baseline: exact output IDs, router digests and finish reasons on all 6 requests. Per request the scheduler issued between 47,654 and 90,095 tickets, adopted 11,161 records in total (4,442 by promoting an in-flight read), refused 24,362 issues at the cap, recorded 0 read failures, and never exceeded the cap (peak live bytes 88,473,600).
