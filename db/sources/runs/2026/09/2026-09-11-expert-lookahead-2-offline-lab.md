Every policy replays the decode demand stream with `ensureCore`'s batch semantics (all keys of a layer event pinned before victims, victims in demand order, no key of the batch evicted) at the native 4,255 slots, at 1.2x (5,106) and at the 24 GB count (5,702), under two seedings (slot order as in the probe; prefill touch order from sweep admissions and prefill demand events plus the snapshot's reference bits). **W1c learned evictor.** Entered because the best implementable policy stayed above 0.85x. `Tools/expert_lookahead_evictor.py train --window 16 --sample 16 --stride 4 --epochs 30` (tool SHA-256 `b4124541c529b89fe16236f7efd2c4b11d20d192c8526ba744699e64bd6114b9`) collected 6,255,248 eviction candidates from a CLOCK replay of the 56 train requests (16 unpinned slots at the hand every fourth decision; label: reused within 16 passes, positive rate 0.562) and fit a logistic scorer over passes since last use, uses in the last 8, 32 and 128 passes, expert frequency rank, used-this-pass and the layer group in 21 s (train AUC 0.736). `evaluate --samples 8,16,32` on validation: misses 0.9907, 0.9727 and 0.9664 of native (hit rates 0.6959, 0.7015, 0.7034; projected 1.0020, 1.0075, 1.0094). It does not reach the 0.85x port threshold and is below segmented LRU, so nothing is ported.

**Token memo probe (W3 offline gate).** `Tools/expert_lookahead_probes.py memo --memo-layers 8` (module `Tools/expert_lookahead_forecast.py`, SHA-256 `f8d7b6b615505f60beaf90443bd56fb06b302c919e677ce034e86cbc830047d0`) over the 1,025 validation passes: the memo's candidates cover 4.23% of all misses (19.0% of the misses of layers 0 to 7) at non-resident precision 0.565 (layer 0: coverage 0.260 at precision 0.791), but under the twin with the lead from pass begin only 1.49% of all misses are covered in time (amplification 1.008, projected 1.0046). The gate (4% timely coverage at precision 0.30) fails on timeliness; the package is dropped.

**Twin calibration with offline stand-in forecasts.** Before the native forecasts exist, `Tools/expert_lookahead_probes.py prefetch-calibration --source offline --split validation --strides 2,1 --per-row-max 16 --memo-layers-max 8 --workers 6` fed the twin with the probe's x2-based forecasts (layer T's router on the captured MoE input of layer T-s), which are a lower bound on the native forecast. The twin models per-row candidates, the margin threshold, the per-target issue cap, the strides and the global cap with exact pass-start residency, counts late-but-demanded tickets separately from wasted ones and releases bytes when the target layer completes. Over 1,025 passes (338,147 misses, 233.6 s of decode, service 0.271 ms per record; margin quantiles q0.25 0.058, q0.50 0.201): best admissible under the 2.0x amplification allowance is stride 1 alone with 10 candidates per row, issue cap 32, cap 32 (timely coverage 0.488, precision 0.401, amplification 1.73, projected 1.1742, 1.1831 with the fixed cost credited); the selection rule's smallest setting within 0.01 is strides 2 then 1 with 8 candidates per row, issue cap 16, cap 32 (coverage 0.475, precision 0.341, amplification 1.92, projected 1.1687); the default stride union with 10 per row and cap 32 projects 1.1926 at amplification 2.26, above the allowance. Sensitivities of the chosen setting: service 1.5x 1.1671, 2.0x 1.1587; advancing deadlines by the saved time 1.1687; adding the memo 1.1733. This stand-in passes the 1.10x rule; the native twin (W3) replaces it once the W2 capture exists.
