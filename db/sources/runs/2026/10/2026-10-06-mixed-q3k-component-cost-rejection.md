# Mixed Q3_K/Q2_0 component cost rejection

This newly considered independent GSQ/RCO release declares the same base checkpoint. It is pinned to [pfeifferj/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-GSQ-RCO-GGUF revision df4f5bbd0a93e5f6a37a377d5d0cf67d89ff0f6e](https://huggingface.co/pfeifferj/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-GSQ-RCO-GGUF/tree/df4f5bbd0a93e5f6a37a377d5d0cf67d89ff0f6e). The authoritative API last-modified date is September 23, not October 6. The publisher's quality uses a different runtime and embedding precision; it does not qualify our custom mixture. Full useful quality and full-checkpoint identity are not tested here.

The exact 12-MiB HTTP206 prefix audit finds 144 expert tensors: 72 Q3_K, ten Q2_K and 62 Q2_0. Their packed payload is 43,332,403,200 bytes, 0.90625 times the rejected GSQ224 native expert bytes. Layer zero has Q3_K gate/up and Q2_0 down, the most common recipe. Hierarchical subgroup scales need a distinct kernel; existing affine admission is not established. The following bounded acquisition reads only layer-zero experts zero through nine, totaling 18,688,000 bytes. Prefix and range hashes are retained, but the published full-shard hash e3af2d65b91481ebb8eb61c4df13f4fba7652eac4cfc7d3d1e4a424f381b6d2c is not independently verified. Binary model inputs remain local with their immutable URL/range/hash coordinates; no weights are redistributed in this record.

The hypothesis is that a direct Q3_K SIMD projection plus the existing Q2_0 down representation could preserve the smaller-byte advantage without dense weight expansion. The frozen cheap screen requires finite reference agreement, row-independent custom reduction, then a complete gate/up/down component at one and three token rows. Each recipe has independent rotating banks of at least 256 MiB. Five alternating rounds each use forty dependent synchronous iterations. Both row counts must cost at most 0.90 times original and at most 1.05 times GSQ224. Failure stops this prototype without implementing Q2_K, a full loader, a full model download or a quality campaign.

The independent oracle is the reviewed Q3_K.dequantize_blocks function from [llama.cpp 4f5406761517648c23dbd60ea5ade37f77a316c9](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/4f5406761517648c23dbd60ea5ade37f77a316c9/gguf-py/gguf/quants.py). The protocol pins its bytes and the C layout/decoder sources. Only that function AST is evaluated, not the upstream module. Q3_K scales are decoded in float32 against BF16 inputs, with BF16 projection output. Q2_0 down scales are explicitly rounded to BF16. Producer quality or original arithmetic is not assumed to transfer.
